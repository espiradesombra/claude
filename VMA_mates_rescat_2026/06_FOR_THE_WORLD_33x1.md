## 3. Inertia without a new war plant: deactivated nuclear + electricity + coolants

### The grid problem (real)

When coal/nuclear baseload leaves the grid, **rotating inertia** falls.  
Frequency drops faster after a fault (**RoCoF**). Batteries help; they are not the only tool.

### The idea (civil reuse — conceptual engineering)

A **deactivated nuclear power plant** is not only “a hole in the map”. It still has:

| Asset | Role for **inertia / buffer** (civil) |
|-------|----------------------------------------|
| **Large pumps, motors, turbines left as rotating machines** | Real **kinetic inertia** if kept spinning or coupled as flywheels / sync condensers-like duty |
| **Electric consumption (gasto eléctrico)** | Controlled load: motors, heaters, pumps — used as **demand-side buffer** and to keep loops alive |
| **Coolant loops / thermal mass (refrigerante / “coolant”)** | Huge **thermal inertia**: heat capacity of water/metal; slow energy in/out via heat exchangers — **not** bomb physics, **heat + flow** |
| **Existing grid connection** | Point of connection already engineered for large power |

**How it rhymes with VMA tech (ZypyZape / Quijote / Kilómetro):**

| VMA piece | Analogy in a dead nuclear site |
|-----------|--------------------------------|
| **ZypyZape** | Several large rotors / pumps **exchange** kinetic energy in cycles; raise **H_eff** of the local grid |
| **Quijote** | Variable inertia / controllable mass or flow (when design allows) — **timing** of buffer charge/discharge |
| **Kilómetro** | Fixed industrial path: fluid loops, long circuits, **mechanical battery** geometry under civil control |

**Honest limits (so this stays with trellat):**

1. This is **not** “free energy from leftover radiation.” Safety, licensing, and decommissioning law come first.  
2. **Electricity spent** on pumps/heaters is **paid energy** — you buy buffer services (ancillary), you don’t print joules from nothing.  
3. Coolant thermal mass is a **slow battery** (minutes–hours), not a magic RoCoF cure alone; combine with **rotating mass** and power electronics.  
4. Same rule as always: **η < 1**, actuators and losses count.  
5. **Civil only.** No dual-use weapons framing.

**One line for the public:**

> *A switched-off nuclear plant can still host **inertia and thermal buffer** if society chooses to reuse motors, electricity, and coolant loops as a **grid battery campus** — the same philosophy as ZypyZape/Quijote/Kilómetro: **orchestrate inertia**, don’t invent perpetual motion.*
